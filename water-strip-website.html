Overview

A coastline strip built for water innovation.

The Water STRIP is a coastline strip along the Red Sea that brings together the main water-sector actors of the region — MEWA, private companies, and leading institutions — to drive commercialization and foster collaboration across the Kingdom's water ecosystem.

Where

The Red Sea coast

A defined corridor running from Rabigh through KAEC and Thuwal to Jeddah, concentrating the region's water assets in one strip.

Who

MEWA, industry & academia

Convened around the Ministry of Environment, Water & Agriculture with private companies and leading universities and research centers.

Why

Commercialize & collaborate

To move water technologies toward commercialization and forge lasting linkages between the actors who can scale them.

Objectives

Three objectives, one water ecosystem.

Everything on the STRIP works toward the goals its members share.

◇

Community Building

Advancing technological solutions that sustainably address the water needs of the Kingdom.

◎

Catalyze Partnerships

Facilitating collaboration and forging linkages amongst members through proactive matchmaking.

✦

Inform Policy

Prioritizing and informing policies that collectively benefit the water ecosystem.

The Journey

Building the Strip, milestone by milestone.

2023

The Strip is founded

Water STRIP launches along the Red Sea coast to connect the region's water actors around MEWA.

First challenge cohort

Anchor members and flagship assets join the corridor from Rabigh through Thuwal to Jeddah.

Commercialization engine
